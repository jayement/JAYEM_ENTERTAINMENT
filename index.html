Featured Artists

AZTER

AZTER is a twelve-member Filipino boy group consists of Tatin, Loyd, Kean, Wayne, Laurence, Miguel, Kl, Cray, Alas, Charlie, Gee, and Gem. They debuted on July 21, 2024, with the single “BINGO“. The group changed its name to AZTER and rebranded in 2025.


source: kprofiles.com

VIVID

Vivid is a four member Filipino girl group consisting of Claire, Chin, Camila and Via. The group is currently signed under B.C Entertainment Production. They will debut on April 24, 2026 with the single “Me So Hot”.



source: kprofiles.com

NXT

NXT is an upcoming 7-member Filipino boy group under Arcana Studios. The members are David, Ten, Zyron, Harvey, Tehll, Jay, and Kyeo. They were formed in October 2025. They released their pre-debut single on February 20, 2026, with their first digital single, “Goody Goody Good”.

source: kprofiles.com
